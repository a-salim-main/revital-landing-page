Does it hurt?

Less than you think. Tiny needles, numbing first — most people say it feels like a flick. Botox takes about five minutes start-to-finish.

How long does it last?

Botox: 3–4 months. Lip flip: 6–8 weeks. Filler: 6–12 months depending on the area. We'll talk through what's realistic for you at your consult.

Am I too young for Botox?

If you're 18+, no. Starting in your 20s with small doses ("preventative" or "baby" Botox) actually slows the formation of lines before they set in. We tailor units to your face, not a chart.
